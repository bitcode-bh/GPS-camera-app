## Searches actually performed

- Exact quoted web queries for `Dikchhavi`, `Bhuvankit`, `Sthanvya`, plus software/camera/trademark queries including spelling variants `Dikchavi`, `Bhuvankith`, `Sthanvia`: no matching software/app brand returned. Search-index coverage is incomplete.
- Official USPTO combined-mark query, all default classes/statuses: `CM:(DIKCHHAVI OR BHUVANKIT OR STHANVYA OR DIKCHAVI OR BHUVANKITH OR STHANVIA)` returned **No results found**. This is a limited wordmark screen, not a phonetic/figurative/common-law clearance opinion.
- Prior non-Indian candidates Locimora, Pixaltro and Stamporyx were screened but superseded by the owner's preference. Do not adopt names from older draft listings without a fresh check.
- IP India OTP/CAPTCHA, WIPO challenge and incomplete EU/other national searches remain the same limitations described in the main assessment. No Indian, worldwide or logo clearance is claimed. Domain/social-handle availability and company-name registration were not checked.
